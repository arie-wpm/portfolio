Lootcentric Metroidvania built in 10 weeks

Built in Unity engine in 10 weeks as a part of a small team, using Jira, Confluence tand git to collaborate. About Me

Hi there, my name is Arie Maljers and I am a game programmer and designer. I have been responsible for systems programming on my flagship title 'Cyberfunk /4/4' and content programming on 'Terminal Horizon'.

In addition to my project work, I achieved a WAM of 85 in my Bachelor of Game Development at the University of Technology Sydney, and have studied abroad at University Sains Malaysia.
